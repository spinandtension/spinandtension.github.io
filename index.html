About

Spin & Tension is a local racquet stringing service based in the heart of Washington, DC. I launched this service after realizing how difficult it is to find reliable stringing in the city center. I began stringing racquets 13 years ago to help pay for my own tennis lessons as a junior and have been stringing for myself and others ever since. My goal is a fast, two-day turnaround—so if you need your racquet strung or want personalized string recommendations, feel free to reach out via email below!

Logistics FAQs

Q: Where are you located?
A: We are based in Capitol Hill, Washington, DC. Exact address details will be provided upon booking.

Q: How long does stringing take?
A: Standard turnaround is about 48 hours depending on workload.

Q: Do you provide strings?
A: Yes—strings are available for purchase, or you can bring your own. I characterize the selection of on-hand strings as small but robust. I have a good breadth of string types, but may not have what you'd like on-hand at the moment. Feel free to reach out and see what I have in stock!

Q: How do I get my racquet to you?
A: This is the most frequently asked question. Most of the raquets that I receive are dropped off at the location of the business. Once you book a restring online or by calling, I will provide better details and time estimates for drop off. Additionally, I have had clients in the past who either couldn't get to me at the pre-arranged time or did not want to brave D.C. traffic (no judgement here!). In those instances, people have opted to use Uber Courier to both send their racquets to me and then have me send their racquets back. Regardless, once you schedule a restring, we will figure something out that works for the both of us and get your racquets ready for the courts!

Synthetics

Synthetic Gut is the cheapest type of string you can buy. It is typically made of nylon and offers good playability for the price. Nothing special, just a good all-around string.

Multifilaments

Offers playability most similar to natural gut string. They are made from hundreds of tiny fibers wound together. Softer on the arm and better for tension maintenance. However, you will sacrifice durability. If you are a big hitter, stay away.

Polyesters

This is the most varied style of string on the market. It can be formed into different edge profiles, providing greater spin while remaining durable. This allows for players to tailor their string specifically to their play style. This is what makes it the most popular among tour professionals. However, you will gain no comfort here. If you know what you are doing then fitting a complementary set of polys to your racquet can really benefit your game!

Natural Gut

Will not lie to you, it is expensive. However, it remains smooth as butter through contact at high tension, allowing fantastic control without having to sacrifice too much on the ole' joints. It also holds its tension very well, meaning restrings are fewer and further between!
